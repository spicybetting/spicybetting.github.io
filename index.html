How to read a card

Pick: half-time result / full-time result. 1 = home team, X = draw, 2 = away team. So X/1 means level at half-time and the home team wins; 2/2 means the away team leads at the break and wins.

System 3/7: seven picks, and the card wins when at least three of them hit.

Under each match: the day, the kick-off (UK time) and, once played, the half-time / full-time score.

green = the pick hit. blue = a hit on one of the harder picks (X/1, X/2, X/X, 1/X, 2/X). Card border: green = won, red = lost, yellow = still running.
